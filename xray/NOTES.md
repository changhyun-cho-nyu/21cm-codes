**H I temperature distribution (`temp_HI_xray.py`, 2026-11-16; `data/temp_HI_xray.{csv,npz,png}`):** H I-mass-weighted pixel `Temperature` (128³, 0.26 cMpc/h pixels; pixel T, not gas cells). Tested on mock renders (planted harmonic means 19.8 / 461.5 K, shares, density bins).
- **Simulation temperature floor: `MinGasTemp = 5` K in both runs** (`data/xray_inspect.txt` lines 81, 690). The 1% H I percentile without X-rays stays at 5.1–5.8 K from z = 29 to 10, instead of falling as (1+z)². The underdense H I (1+δ < 1) is at 20 K at z = 29, the adiabatic IGM value (2.7255 (1+z)²/125.8 = 19.5 K); carried adiabatically, mean-density gas would be ~10 K at z = 20, 5.7 K at 15 and 2.7 K at 10. So from z ≈ 15 the floor prevents unheated gas from cooling as it should. For coupled gas at z = 10, 5 K instead of 2.7 K halves 1 − T_γ/T_K (−5 vs −10). In pixels the floor touches only the coldest few % of the H I (8–12% below 10 K); in gas cells possibly more. It limits the no-X-ray absorption, so it is another reason the X-ray difference is a lower bound. (Lumina's MinGasTemp not checked here.)
