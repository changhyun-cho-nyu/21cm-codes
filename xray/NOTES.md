Separate from the Lumina fiducial pipeline. Everything specific to these runs is in this directory and is run **from this
directory** (`cd xray`); the shared pipeline code (`../compute_Jalpha*.c`, `../make_SFR_eff.py`, `../make_Z_snapshots.py`,

(compile, then submit) → 21 cm maps (to come). Data from the inspection: `data/`.

For the paper: 39–53% of the injected X-ray energy is in the 0.5–2 keV bin, which contains the photons whose heating ĉ = 0.2c reduces; the X-ray effect is a lower bound (size not quantified: the bin is grey in the RT, one cross-section per bin, and its 0.5–1 keV part is absorbed within about a Hubble time even at ĉ). Mean free paths in neutral gas at z ≈ 8 (formula below): 0.5 cMpc at 100 eV, 4 at 200, 14 at 300, 63 at 500, ~500 at 1 keV, ~4000 at 2 keV. So the 100–200 eV heating (mostly hot ISM) is patchy around galaxies, 200–500 eV reaches box scales, 0.5–2 keV (mostly HMXB) is near-uniform over the 50 cMpc box. (Corrected in the 2026-11-15 review: "100–500 eV within ~2–15 cMpc" was wrong at the upper end.)
- **Reduced speed of light: `RSLAFactor` = 0.2** (ĉ = 0.2c; `CLIGHT_RT` in `data/constants.h:120`, used for all RT in `MRT/RT.cc:104`; the only c/ĉ correction found is the photoelectric-heating factor `fac_pe`, `RT_thermochem.cc:351`; no X-ray-specific correction). Rates are Γ = ĉ n_γ σ: photons absorbed within much less than a Hubble time are unaffected (absorption delayed by 1/0.2), photons with mean free path ≳ ĉ/H (~500 cMpc at z ≈ 8) deposit 0.2 of their true heating. λ_X ≈ 4.9 x_HI^{-1/3} ((1+z)/15)^{-2} (E/300 eV)³ cMpc (Furlanetto, Oh & Briggs 2006): only the 500–2000 eV bin is affected. The early estimate "bias ≲ 5%" assumed AGN-only X-rays and is superseded: HMXBs dominate the 0.5–2 keV bin (budget above).
