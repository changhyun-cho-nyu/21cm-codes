## 1. 21 cm spin temperature: done

**What changed: `SPIN_MODEL=1`, in `spin_wf.h` and `PS_21cm_mpi.c`.** It is the default from 2026-11-15:
1. τ_GP from first principles, (π e² f λ / m_e c) n_HI / H, with the pixel's own n_HI. The old 3×10⁵ x_HI ((1+z)/7)^{3/2} was 1.30× low for Lumina.
2. Hirata (2006) S̃_α and colour temperature T_c ≠ T_K.
3. Venumadhav et al. (2018): x_CMB = (1 − e^{−τ₂₁})/τ₂₁ in T_S, and δT_b × x_CMB (the exact 1 − e^{−τ₂₁}).
4. T_S by fixed-point iteration, with damping after 100 steps. A pixel with 1/T_c ≤ 0 (T_K ≲ 0.4 K) stops the run.

`-DSPIN_MODEL=0` reproduces the 2026-10-15 maps bit for bit (tested).

**Production: job 29237390.**
- 305/305 maps in `/orcd/data/mvogelsb/004/chcho/Tb_maps_prod/H_real_wf`, verified with `check_Tb_maps.py` (worst cell 2.1×10⁻⁵).
- The old maps stay in `Tb_maps_prod/H_real`.
- Global signal, new vs old: +17% (+0.16 mK) at z = 15, +7% at z = 17, +2.5% at z = 13, ≤ 0.5% at z ≤ 12.
- x_CMB ≥ 0.987 in every pixel.
- The mechanism is Hirata's T_c (it reduces absorption by cold gas), which matters only while x̄_α ~ 0.05–1 (z ≈ 16–12.7).

**Independent code review** (two reviewers; `WF_PHYSICS.md` §6):

  - `run_21cm.sh`: fixed NTASK = 8, compile flags stored in `bin/PS_21cm_mpi.flags`, OUT_DIR tied to SPIN_MODEL;

**`tb_physics.Line(..., spin_model=1)` is now the default.** Pass `spin_model=0` (or `--spin-model 0`) to reproduce results made before 2026-11-15.

  - **The "warm neutral voids" seen in the pixels are sub-pixel mixing.** Underdense neutral cells sit at the simulation's 5 K floor (`MinGasTemp = 5` in both runs).
  - About 33% of all neutral H I is below 5.5 K. Adiabatic cooling would give 3.4–4.7 K now and ~1.5–2.6 K at z = 10.
  - Overdense neutral gas (1+δ > 1, ~half the H I) is really heated: 79–1000 K.
  - The 128³ pixels overstate ⟨1/T⟩⁻¹ (which a coupled T_S follows) 2–5×. So the no-X-ray absorption in the pixel maps is far too shallow, consistent with the "z ≳ 10 maps are upper limits" rule.
  - The two runs are identical at z = 15.
